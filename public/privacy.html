Privacy Policy

RC Pilates & Fitness Studio • Effective Date: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RC Pilates & Fitness Studio ("we," "our," or "the Studio"). We value your trust and are dedicated to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you access our member portal and mobile applications.

2. Information We Collect

When you register and use the RC Pilates platform, we may collect the following:

  • Google Account Information: Name, email address, and profile photo provided via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0).
  • Studio Member Details: Age, phone number, emergency contacts, medical/injury history, and fitness goals to tailor your pilates training.
  • Session & Attendance Records: Booked batch time slots, attendance marks, and renewal dates.
  • Membership Tier Information: Package selection (e.g., 1-Month or 3-Month memberships) and fee transaction status.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate studio management purposes, including:

  • Authenticating your member identity and securing your account.
  • Scheduling reformer and mat pilates sessions and assigning batch slots.
  • Allowing studio instructors to monitor workout safety and accommodate physical conditions.
  • Sending schedule reminders, renewal notices, and studio announcements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing advertisers. Your information is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud for authentication) solely to deliver our services.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile data as long as your membership is active. You may request account deletion or data removal at any time by speaking with the lead instructor or contacting us at the email below.
